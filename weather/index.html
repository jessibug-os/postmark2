Rain belongs to what it touches.

Weather is no longer a camera filter and one universal ring. It falls through the world’s authored surface truth, leaves different evidence on water and porous ground, changes the island’s light and sound, and remains after the cloud has gone.

The rain pipeline

A streak does not decide what the ground is by color. The authored island and tagged assets answer that question, then Weather chooses the smallest response that communicates the material.

1Seed a streakOne of 300 varied line segments appears 8–20 units above the player inside a 28-unit field.
2Resolve destinationThe island field supplies ground height, water height, terrain and local slope normal.
3Test interceptionA cached list of tagged roofs and foliage may replace the terrain as the first surface hit.
4Express materialWater rings, porous dark marks, hard pinpricks or canopy absorption—not one universal splash.
5Leave weather behindCell moisture darkens terrain, fills local authored hollows, and drains in broad uneven patches.

Two clocks, thousands of local values

Intensity answers “is weather happening now?” Each cell’s moisture answers “what happened at this patch recently?” Deterministic smooth noise varies the rates, so wetness advances in broad patches rather than a uniform wipe or random speckles.

Noise varies coverage; authors still place puddles

Two smooth noise octaves are sampled once per cell. They vary wetting and drying rates, never terrain identity or puddle location. Neighbouring cells tend to agree; distant patches diverge.

The three civic hollows still have authored thresholds of 32%, 50% and 68%, but each now reads moisture at its own world position. Two runoff strips do the same at the well roof.

After rain: streaks stop first. Faster-draining cells recover while darker patches and local puddles remain.

The whole island responds

Rain has to agree across geometry, color, depth and sound. Each cue is restrained; their agreement is what makes the weather feel present.

Light and distance

  • Rain draws the fog far plane toward 110 units and cools fog/background slightly.
  • Cloud cover reduces directional light by up to 24%, hemisphere light by 12%, and ambient fill by 8%.
  • The character-only rim remains untouched, preserving the “player always visible” rule.
  • At full soak, river, ocean and waterfall colors darken by approximately 12%.

Place-sensitive ambience

  • Soft ground filters the rain near 1120 Hz.
  • Water opens the sound around 1750 Hz.
  • Roof, timber and stone sharpen the patter around 2450 Hz and raise it slightly.
  • Birds and crickets hush. Interiors remove direct weather ambience and heavily muffle the outside world.
Wind coherence today: all rain strokes share a direction and horizontal speed. Foliage, laundry, clouds and smoke do not yet consume that same vector, so the coherent wind is real inside precipitation but not yet universal.

Console contract

grid -w turns on an 11×11 gameplay-cell window centered on the player. Every visible cell carries its rounded numerical wetness label, while its fill blends from ivory toward patina according to that same value. The local window follows the player and refreshes when wetness crosses five-percentage-point steps.

What the system does not claim

These are boundaries of the current implementation, not hidden future-tense features.

Lightweight moisture transport

Once per second, slopes shed excess to their lowest neighbour and, after rain, level cells exchange moisture toward drier outlets. This is grid hydrology, not a fluid simulation with depth, channels or erosion.

No puddle gameplay

Puddles are shallow visual decals: no reflection, slowdown, footsteps or save state.

No secondary canopy drops

Foliage catches rain but does not twitch, accumulate water or shed larger drops.

Simple runoff

Eave streams are authored translucent strips, not droplet chains following roof topology.

Tagged interception

Future imported buildings and furniture need a rain tag or recognizable name to catch drops.

One synthesized ambience

Material sound is a filtered noise source, not a library of authored roof, leaf, mud and water recordings.

Impact draw calls

Transient marks are capped at 52 but are not yet one instanced draw call.

Snow is narrower

Snow respects local ground height but does not yet share rain’s full material/interceptor system.
